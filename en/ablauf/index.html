Process & preparation

A good move
starts with preparation.

This preparation helps you clearly describe the work, process and open questions when discussing the job later.

A summary
to take with you.

Enter the essentials in the planner, then check your details and save them as a text file or print them.

01 / List everything that will come along.

Make a list by room or category. Record quantities, dimensions and weight. Mark fragile, valuable or particularly heavy items. Photos may help with later discussions; this preview does not upload files.

02 / Check the whole route to the door.

Check both locations: floors, lifts, door widths, stairs and the route from vehicle to entrance. An available lift must also suit the load and the building's rules.

  • Where can the vehicle actually park?
  • Are there time restrictions on access or loading areas?
  • Is a temporary no-parking zone needed? Blocking a space with personal belongings does not replace official permission.

03 / Agree on scope and responsibilities.

Before a real job is commissioned, services, date, price, extra costs, liability and responsibilities must be clearly agreed. This includes personnel, packing, dismantling and any special transport equipment needed.

In this preview, the process ends with your own summary. A request recipient, quotation and binding booking process have not yet been set up.

04 / Prepare for the handover.

Pack securely, label items by destination room and keep routes clear. Check the load together at collection and arrival; record any issues objectively in a handover report. Keep personal documents and valuables separately with you.
