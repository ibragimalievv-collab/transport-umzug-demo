Missing detailsService informationConsumer information
Incomplete project details. Before the public business launch, all applicable statutory information must be complete and checked.

What needs to be added before launch.

The responsible person and future service provider have not been named. There is also no address for service or genuine electronic contact information. These gaps are stated explicitly rather than filled with invented details.

  • Full name or company name, legal form, an address for service and, where relevant, an authorised representative.
  • A genuine email address and a way to communicate directly and promptly.
  • Register and registration number, VAT identification number or business identification number where applicable and available. A private tax number must not be published as a substitute.
  • The competent supervisory authority and further details where required by the activity.

The main legal starting point: § 5 DDG. Restricting access does not automatically remove all legal obligations of the actual operator.

Services and supporting documents.

The fleet, payloads, insurance cover and permits have not yet been determined. No existing Community licence or insurance cover is claimed. Before transport operations begin, the vehicle, trailer, route type and service model must be checked against the current GüKG and applicable EU requirements.

The legal position depends on the actual activity. Changes to licensing rules in 2026 and transitional provisions for earlier permits must be taken into account: § 3 GüKG, § 24 GüKG.

Consumer information for the actual process.

Dispute resolution information under § 36 VSBG and any other applicable rules will be added based on the company details and duties. A link to the discontinued EU online dispute resolution platform is not included here.

This preview has no online booking. If contracts can later be concluded through the site, information duties and withdrawal rights must be assessed separately. For goods transport on a specific date or during a specific period, § 312g Abs. 2 Nr. 9 BGB provides an exception. It must not be applied indiscriminately to every offer. The electronic withdrawal function in force since 19 June 2026 must be considered for relevant online distance contracts carrying a right of withdrawal.

Whether the BFSG applies to the future service, and whether a microenterprise exemption is available, must be assessed using the actual business model and company size. The preview is designed with keyboard operation, visible focus, form labels and reduced motion; this is not a certificate of full compliance.

A final review using the actual company and contract details is required before trading begins.
