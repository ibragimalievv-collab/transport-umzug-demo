Project status
Can I already book a transport?

Not yet. The business is in preparation. The planner creates a summary on your device. Nothing is submitted, no date is reserved and no contract is concluded.

Which details help with planning?

Collection and destination locations, an inventory, dimensions and weight, floors, lifts and carrying distances. For a move, the number of boxes and a realistic time window also help.

What makes up a transport price?

For example, distance and journey time, load volume and weight, staffing, access and additional services. A reliable price requires a review of the specific details. No prices are calculated in this preview.

What is the volume estimate for?

It is a rough planning aid based on fixed values per item. Actual dimensions, packing and stackability may differ significantly. It does not check weight, payload or vehicle choice.

Are assembly and packing available?

You can note the help you need in the planner. Whether a service will actually be offered and carried out must be confirmed individually after the business launches. Electrical, water or gas connections are not promised.

What matters for heavy or fragile items?

Clarify weight, dimensions, material, packaging and the entire route beforehand. Pianos, safes, artwork, dangerous goods and particularly heavy items require a separate suitability assessment; this preview does not confirm acceptance.

Where is my input stored?

The application holds your input only in the open page's working memory. Reloading resets the planner. A file or printout is created for you only if you download or print the summary. Hosting and access services may process technical data when you visit the site.

Which towns will be served?

The base and service area have not yet been defined. Location fields help you prepare your job and do not confirm that a route is available. Actual locations and served regions will be added once established.
