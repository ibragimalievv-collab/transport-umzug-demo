01 / Furniture transport

A piece that is
ready to move on.

The sofa from your living room. A second-hand wardrobe. The dining table for your new home. Even a single piece of furniture deserves a well-planned journey.

Plan furniture transport
What matters

A favourite piece. A new place.

For individual furniture items, consider dimensions, protection and access through the stairwell, whether it is a newly bought sofa or a large wardrobe.

Essential detailsPrepare wellAgree in advanceTo the planner ↗

What matters for planning.

  • The furniture's length, width and height in its intended transport condition.
  • The weight or a reasonable weight estimate.
  • Delicate surfaces, glass, loose parts and existing packaging.
  • Door widths, stair landings and lift dimensions at both locations.

Measure before space gets tight.

Measure the route too: the building entrance, narrowest door, turns on the stairs and usable space inside the lift. A small volume does not mean that a piece of furniture will fit through every opening.

What to clarify in advance

  • Will the furniture stay assembled, or does it need dismantling?
  • Who provides suitable blankets, corner protection and packing materials?
  • Who helps carry at collection and delivery?
  • Are there delivery times or access rules to observe?

Good to know

The planner estimates volume using fixed guide values. The actual dimensions matter more for a specific piece of furniture. Particularly heavy, valuable or fragile items require a separate assessment.
