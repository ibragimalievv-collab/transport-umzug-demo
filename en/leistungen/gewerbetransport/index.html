What matters

Keep your business moving.

Loading conditions and time windows matter for office furniture, work materials and other business goods.

Essential detailsPrepare wellAgree in advanceTo the planner ↗

What matters for planning.

  • The type, number, dimensions and weight of the goods; pallet dimensions where relevant.
  • Collection and delivery time windows, and site access rules.
  • Available loading ramps, forklifts or other loading equipment needed.
  • Special requirements, reference numbers and required handover documents.

Understand on-site operations first.

Can loading take place during opening hours? Is advance registration required? Who handles the handover? These questions help ensure that planning goes beyond the entrance gate.

Also clarify for an office move

  • Clearly label workstations and destination rooms.
  • Secure IT equipment in a way that protects data and use suitable packaging.
  • Agree on downtime, room access and internal responsibilities.
  • Agree separately on disposal, storage and IT connections.

Check service limitations

Pallet, part-load and full-load services, specialist transport and dangerous goods have not been confirmed as available. The eventual service range depends on vehicles, permits and actual suitability.
