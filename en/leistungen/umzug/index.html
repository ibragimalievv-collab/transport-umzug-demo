One home.
A new beginning.

Moving home is more than a journey. Inventory, packing, access and timing all work together. A clear overview makes the next arrangements easier.

Plan your move
What matters

A home moves on.

A move starts with a good list. Rooms, furniture, boxes and access make the work easier to understand.

Essential detailsPrepare wellAgree in advanceTo the planner ↗

What matters for planning.

  • An inventory by room, including large furniture and appliances.
  • A realistic count and size of packed boxes.
  • Floors, lifts, carrying distances and access restrictions.
  • A preferred date or flexible time window, and any assembly needs.

Room by room, rather than a rough guess.

Go through every room. Remember the cellar, attic, balcony and garage. Clearly separate what is coming along from items to sell or give away beforehand.

Before moving day

  • Pack boxes securely, keep their weight manageable and label them by destination room.
  • Keep important documents, keys, medicines and valuables separately with you.
  • Prepare refrigerators and machinery for transport according to the manufacturer's instructions.
  • Agree on key handover, access and, if needed, an officially authorised no-parking zone.

Agree on additional services

Packing, dismantling and assembly must be agreed separately. Electrical, water or gas connections may require qualified specialists. Selecting a need in the planner is not a service commitment.
