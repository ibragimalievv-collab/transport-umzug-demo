What matters

A small load. Plenty to consider.

A few boxes, a table or a bulky find: even a small load needs clear arrangements.

Essential detailsPrepare wellAgree in advanceTo the planner ↗

What matters for planning.

  • The number, dimensions and weight of each package.
  • Whether the load is stackable, fragile or particularly bulky.
  • The precise handover point: kerbside, front door or carrying inside the building.
  • The preferred time window and loading and unloading conditions.

Small does not always mean light.

A compact machine may weigh more than a large wardrobe. A volume estimate therefore does not replace weight information or checks on payload and load securing.

Helpful details to agree on

  • List packages clearly and secure loose parts.
  • Tell the seller or collection point about the intended time window.
  • Check parking access and short carrying routes.
  • State whether loading and unloading assistance is needed.

No automatic express promise

The vehicle, route and date must be explicitly confirmed later. This preview promises neither immediate collection nor a particular delivery time.
