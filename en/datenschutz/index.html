Information about the private technical preview. The controller and full contact details have not yet been named. This page is therefore not yet a complete notice under Article 13 GDPR.

Your input stays in the page's working memory.

The planner does not ask for names, phone numbers or email addresses. Please do not enter personal, confidential or sensitive information in free-text fields. A town or postcode is enough; an exact home address is not needed.

The application uses no database, cookies or browser storage for form input. Your details are held only in the open page's working memory and reset on reload. There is no submission function or automatic request to a transport provider.

“Download summary” creates a text file on your device. The print function lets you make a printout or save a PDF through your system dialog. You manage these files and printouts yourself. “Clear input” resets the current plan.

Technical data when visiting.

This preview is independently hosted on GitHub Pages. When pages load, GitHub Pages and the network services involved may process technical information such as IP address, request time, device information and logs. No platform account is required to view the site. The planner sends no entries to a transport provider.

The responsible person has not yet been named. Before commercial use, the legal bases, specific recipients, retention periods and any international transfers must be completed for the actual configuration. Hosting provider information: GitHub Pages privacy policy.

No added tracking services.

The website code embeds no advertising, analytics, remote fonts, maps or social widgets. The application sets no cookies of its own and uses neither Local Storage nor Session Storage. Switching language loads only an own-origin static page; form entries are not sent.

Before adding services, assess whether consent is required under section 25 TDDDG or the GDPR. The absence of a request submission feature does not mean that hosting processes no technical data.

Before accepting real enquiries.

  • Name the controller and provide reachable privacy contact details.
  • Document purposes, specific legal bases, data categories, recipients and processors.
  • Check hosting, email and form services; establish required agreements, retention periods, deletion procedures and safeguards.
  • Explain data subject rights, the right to complain and the competent supervisory authority.
  • Clearly describe any international transfers and safeguards where relevant.
  • Only then connect a real request channel and test it with harmless sample data.

Legal starting points: GDPR (DSGVO) and § 25 TDDDG. The final privacy notice must describe how the service actually operates.
